Origin story

When I was 8 years old, I asked my parents to buy a second-hand Nintendo if I got all A's on my report card. Since then, gaming has been a huge part of my life.

I've also been a huge fan of computers, and when my Dad brought home an IBM-XT, a whole new world of computing opened up for me. Running games via DOS and editing memory config files so games could run were all part of my digital childhood. It wouldn't be until later that those commands and that gaming would turn into a passion for crafting images and games, but the foundations were laid by the chip tunes of the 8-bit days.

career.log_ x

Professional

My first REAL programming job was at a company called Omation. At the time, I had graduated from FullSail learning Maya for 3D, and most of my scripting background was MEL (Maya Embedded Language). However, when I was hired my boss wanted me to code in JavaScript for Softimage XSI! So for the first two weeks we printed out all the JavaScript code, taped it to our glass cubicle doors, and stared at it until it made sense. Ridiculous! From there I scripted and rigged all of the props on a feature animation called Barnyard, and thus began my professional career. I've been solving problems with computers professionally ever since.

At the time of publishing, I've worked on feature animation, game cinematics, commercial VFX, and now video games on League of Legends with Riot Games. I've served as a rigging tech artist, a 3D generalist, a Houdini FX Artist, a Creative Director, and now a Principal Tech Artist on one of the world's most popular MOBAs. I'm grateful for the journey over the years, and my passion for technology, code, art, and the craft of making games continues to grow.

Indie aspirations

I'm always exploring new technologies, and game engines are some of the most incredible pieces of software in my opinion. They unlock creative ideas across all disciplines like art, programming, music, and writing. So it is no surprise that one day I would love to craft an indie game of my own with a few close friends.

I'm learning Unity and C# on the side today to build the dev muscles that will be needed tomorrow. As the gaming industry continues to evolve and grow, I look forward to growing with it and working alongside passionate developers and collaborators from all over the world.
